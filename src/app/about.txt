GameLord's Audio Player (GLAP) is an audio player, written in Rust. Why? Why not. I like rust, so I thought "Audio player? MAKE IT IN RUST!" and it led to this.

Credits:
 - RataTUI for a nicely structured TUI library.
 - Symphonia for a easy to use audio parser.
 - SDL2 for a simple and direct media layer.

FAQ:
 - Q: "Why can't I see the play/pause button, forward/back buttons, etc?" A: Make sure that you have a Nerd Font installed to your computer and as a fallback or main font for your terminal.
 - Q: "Why does the time to pausing vs the audio stopping feel the same as Windows Media Player / VLC / whatever I use?" A: Because it probably is the exact same because of how SDL2 works.
